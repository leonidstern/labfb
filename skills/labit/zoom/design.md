# Zoom: design

For blocks that are a look, a screen, a component, a drawing, or copy on a screen.

**Bigger picture.** What this is a piece of:
- the screen and the flow it sits in: what the person is doing there, before and after
- the design system rules it follows (tokens, type, spacing, components), and where it breaks them
- the parts next to it that must match it
- what was asked for, picked and dropped on the way (from the trail)

**Deeper.** Into this one piece:
- its states: hover, pressed, focus, empty, loading, error, long text, phone width, dark
- its details: sizes, spacing, type, colour, as the tokens they are
- when the person is exploring looks: 3 or 4 versions side by side within the same idea, each with one visible difference named under it
- what doesn't work yet, seen by looking at it

**Rules.** Use the page's own tokens and parts; copy the screen's patterns, invent no new badges or cards. Draw it and look at it before handing it over (render, screenshot). A drawing stays that drawing.

**Deliver.** Show, don't describe: the states or versions drawn, each with a one-line caption.
