# Zoom: product and ideas

For blocks that are an idea, a plan, a bet, a decision or a product choice: where a lead or a CEO looks.

**Bigger picture.** What this sits in:
- the goal or problem it is for, and for whom
- the other bets aimed at the same goal, and how this one compares
- what it costs (time, people, money) and what it blocks or unblocks
- what was decided and dropped on the way, and why (from the trail and the lab's other pages)

**Deeper.** Into this one idea:
- how it would work, step by step, for the person using it
- the smallest first version and what it leaves out
- the risks, and how each would show early
- what to measure to know it works
- the open questions, each with who can answer it

**Rules.** Decision-shaped: lead with what matters for deciding. No made-up market numbers; mark guesses as guesses.

**Deliver.** Short points; a small table when comparing bets.
