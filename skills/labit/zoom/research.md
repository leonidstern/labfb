# Zoom: research

For blocks that are a finding, a quote, a study, a source or an insight.

**Bigger picture.** What this finding is part of:
- the question the research set out to answer
- all the findings next to this one, and how strong each is
- what this one changes, and what is still open
- what is missing to answer the question

**Deeper.** Under this one finding:
- the evidence: real quotes from the project's files, with who and where
- how many say it, and who says the opposite
- how it was found, and the limits of that method
- what would change the conclusion

**Rules.** Every quote is real and findable in the files. Counts are counted, not estimated. Say plainly when the evidence is thin.

**Deliver.** Quotes as quotes, with their source; a short line on what they add up to.
