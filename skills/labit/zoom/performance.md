# Zoom: performance

For blocks about speed, size, memory, load time, a benchmark or any measured number.

**Bigger picture.** Where this number sits in the whole:
- the goal or budget it is measured against, and whether it's met
- the whole path it is one part of (the full page load, the full request), and this part's share of it
- what a person actually feels from it, on the devices they use
- the numbers next to it, and which one matters most right now
- what was decided about it and dropped on the way (from the trail)

**Deeper.** Underneath the number:
- measure it again now, the same way it was measured, and say if it moved
- break it down: where the time, bytes or memory go, largest first
- the conditions that change it (slow phone, many items, a second visit, a cold start), measured, not guessed
- what was tried and what came of it, from the trail
- what is left to do, with the gain each would bring
- what is not known: engines, devices or sizes nobody measured

**Rules.** Never invent a number. Measure it, or quote it from the page, the trail or a file, and say which. Give the method in one line (tool, device, runs). Differences inside run-to-run noise are noise: say so. Keep machines calm: one short run, not a suite, unless asked.

**Deliver.** A table for the numbers, a few points for what they mean.
